    Expected: L1 and L2 miss the moved slot, and L3 re-aims too fast. In testing (headless Chrome, 1400×1000) the background check vetoed none of the four: they scored about 0.4–0.7, under its 0.87 line. L1's score depends on the page layout and can land near the line. That line was set for a 1% human false-positive budget, and it is the Tier 4 result live: passive statistics can be copied, which is why the puzzle exists. L4 gets in — a bot that models human reaction delay passes. The slot is only ever drawn into the board image among decoys, so every bot here has to find it by looking at pixels. That costs the attacker a perception step (its measured cost is shown above), not a wall. The puzzle raises the cost of an attack; it does not make one impossible. All puzzle thresholds are hand-set, not calibrated on real users.
